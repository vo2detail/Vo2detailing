FAQ

What customers usually ask.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes. VO2 Detailing arrives with professional cleaning equipment, chemicals and detailing tools. Access needs can be confirmed when booking.

How long does a detail take?

Most services take several hours depending on vehicle size, condition and the selected package. You’ll receive an estimate before the appointment.

Can pricing change after I book?

The quote is based on the information and photos provided. If the vehicle’s condition is significantly different, any pricing change will be discussed before additional work begins.

Where do you provide service?

VO2 Detailing is focused on Seattle and the greater Pacific Northwest. Exact availability depends on location and schedule.
